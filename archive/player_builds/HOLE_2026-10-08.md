- **Skills** (32 XP): Acrobatics 3, Brawl 2, Stealth 2, Awareness 1,
  Might 1, Resilience 2, Insight 2, Survival 2, Composure 3, Theurgy 2.

## What the link leaves out

The compact share link only carries Techniques, Stats and Skills: no
items or armor, no backgrounds, no per-Technique choices (Soulblade's
weapon type, Temper Soulblade's Level and Masterwork power), no XP
budget. An Export file from the Builder carries all of that.

## Derived numbers (rulebook formulas)

- Health 10 (5 Shallow, 5 Deep), Speed 3, Reflex 3, cards a day 4.
- Skill Totals: Acrobatics 5, Brawl 4, Melee 2, Resilience 5,
  Composure 4, Theurgy 4, Insight 3, Might 4, Awareness 4.
- Vital Defense 13, Mental 12, Vigilant 11, Dodge 13 (Acrobatics).
- If Soulblade makes a 2H Light Melee weapon (Melee or Brawl, so Brawl
  here): attack +5, Damage 4 + Body = 7, Parry 13.

## Where the XP went

Clearly outside combat: Spacious Gut, Gorger and Vow of Poverty (12 XP),
plus Survival and Theurgy with no Theurgy Technique (6). Partly combat:
Stealth, Insight, Awareness, Composure (Composure is Soulblade's prereq
and Mental Defense). Roughly a quarter of the XP is outside combat,
inside the designer's two-thirds-to-four-fifths combat guideline.

For comparison, the sim's Level 2 sample characters (125 XP) attack at
Skill Total 7–9; HOLE's best attack is 4 (+1 from the weapon).
